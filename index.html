Exit Nodes

What is an Exit Node?

An exit node is a server on the Tailscale network where your internet traffic "leaves" the private network. Think of it like choosing which door you walk out of before stepping onto the internet.

When you connect through a Guruji exit node, your apps and browser talk to the internet as if they were sitting at that node's location — while the traffic itself stays encrypted to and from your device.

No device sees the others' data, and you can switch exit nodes or disconnect entirely in the Tailscale app at any time.

📶 How connection works

Device → Tailscale mesh → chosen exit node → internet. One tap in the app to switch or disconnect.

🤝 How sharing works

Exit nodes are shared across members. Your subscription determines how many of your devices can connect at once.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an Exit Node? ＋

An exit node is a server on the network that your internet traffic passes through before reaching the public internet. It's where your traffic "exits" — giving you that server's viewpoint of the web while your connection stays encrypted.

How does Guruji VPN work? ＋

We use Tailscale, a mesh VPN built on WireGuard. After you sign in, your device is part of a private network. When you enable an exit node, your encrypted traffic routes through that node to the internet. The dashboard shows your connection status and lets you manage devices.

How do I connect my phone? ＋

Install Tailscale from the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android), sign in with your email, then open the app menu → Exit Node → choose Guruji VPN India → connect. Done in under two minutes.

How many devices can I use? ＋

Trial: 1 device. Monthly: 3 devices. Yearly: 5 devices. All your devices sign in with the same account and show up in your dashboard.

What happens after payment? ＋

Your payment is verified on the server with PhonePe, your subscription is activated instantly, and your dashboard reflects the new plan and expiry date. Then install Tailscale and connect your devices.

How do I disconnect? ＋

In the Tailscale app, tap or click the active exit node and turn it off — your connection returns to your normal internet. You can also use tailscale down in a terminal.
