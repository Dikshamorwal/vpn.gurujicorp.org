Troubleshooting

Tailscale account kaise banayein?
Upar diye gaye 5 steps dekhein: login.tailscale.com par jaakar apne Gmail se sign in karein.
VPN not connecting
Restart Tailscale service or run tailscale down followed by tailscale up.
Exit node unavailable
Verify that your subscription is active in the Dashboard and re-authenticate your Tailscale client.
Slow connection speed
Switch to a geographically closer node (e.g., India or Singapore) for optimal ping and latency.
